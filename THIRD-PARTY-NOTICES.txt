FaultWitness Windows x64 self-contained distribution
Third-party redistribution inventory

This inventory describes third-party components observed in an untrimmed,
multi-file win-x64 publish of FaultWitness. Versions are the resolved
package/runtime versions for that publish. This is an inventory, not a legal
opinion. The upstream package notice/license files linked below are the
authoritative notices and should be retained with any redistributed build.

UI and graphics
---------------
* Avalonia 12.1.1 (Avalonia, Avalonia.Desktop, Avalonia.Themes.Fluent and
  their resolved platform assemblies): MIT. Source: https://github.com/AvaloniaUI/Avalonia/
* SkiaSharp 3.119.4 and SkiaSharp.NativeAssets.Win32 3.119.4 (SkiaSharp.dll,
  libSkiaSharp.dll): MIT. The package's bundled third-party notice covers
  Skia, HarfBuzz, ANGLE, and other native graphics material:
  https://www.nuget.org/packages/SkiaSharp.NativeAssets.Win32/3.119.4
* HarfBuzzSharp 8.3.1.3 and Avalonia.HarfBuzz 12.1.1
  (HarfBuzzSharp.dll, libHarfBuzzSharp.dll): MIT / upstream HarfBuzz notices.
  Source: https://github.com/mono/harfbuzzsharp (package metadata); upstream:
  https://github.com/harfbuzz/harfbuzz
* ANGLE native assets (av_libglesv2.dll), resolved through Avalonia.Angle.Windows.Natives
  2.1.27548.20260419: BSD-style license. The package includes the required
  copyright, redistribution conditions, and disclaimer:
  https://www.nuget.org/packages/Avalonia.Angle.Windows.Natives/2.1.27548.20260419
